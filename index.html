GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is Wishora really free?

Yes. Every design, theme, sticker, reveal and sharing option is free. There are no locked templates, no watermarks to pay off and no sign-up.

How does the person receive my wish?

When you tap a share button, Wishora creates a link that contains your wish. When they open it, they see the surprise reveal first, then your card with its animation, music button and countdown. You can also download the card as an image and send that instead.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Photos are read by your own browser and never uploaded. That's why they appear in your preview and in downloaded images, but aren't included in shared links yet.

Can I write in Urdu, Hindi or Arabic?

Yes. Type in any language. Right-to-left text is detected automatically, and you can choose the Urdu Nastaliq font in the Design tab.

How do I post my wish to Instagram?

Tap the Instagram button. On phones that support it, your card opens in the share sheet so you can pick Instagram. Otherwise it's saved as an image, which you can add to your Story or send in a DM.

Is my draft saved?

Your text and design choices are saved on this device automatically, so a refresh won't lose your work. Tap “Start over” to clear it.
