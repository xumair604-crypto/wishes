Privacy policy

Wishora is built so that your wishes stay yours. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the things you type and upload.

What we don't collect

You don't need an account. Wishora does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and the current version has no server that stores your wishes.

Your photos

Photos you add are read by your own browser and shown in your preview and in images you download. They are not uploaded to Wishora.

Your draft

Your text and design choices are saved in your browser's local storage on your device, so a refresh doesn't lose your work. Tap “Start over” or clear your browser data to remove it.

Shared wish links

When you share a wish, its text and design settings are packed into the link itself (after the # sign). Anyone who has the link can open the wish, so only share it with people you trust. The part after # is not sent to our web server.

Third-party services

Wishora loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you add a YouTube link, the video is shown using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. When you share to WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram, Instagram or email, those apps' own privacy policies apply.

Children

Wishora is intended for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If we add features such as accounts or cloud storage, we'll update this page first. Questions? Contact us at hello@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.
